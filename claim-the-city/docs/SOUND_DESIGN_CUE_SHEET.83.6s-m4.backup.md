## 3. Spotting Summary & Density Discipline

- **Total Distinct Essential SFX Hits:** **15 placements** across `83.60s` (average ~1 tactile cue every `5.5s` during active gameplay, **0 cues** during `S01–S02` setup and `S08` warung reunion), fulfilled using **12 compact reusable sound assets** (or **10** using the lean minimum kit in [`docs/AUDIO_ASSET_REQUIREMENTS.md`](./AUDIO_ASSET_REQUIREMENTS.md)), with `sfx_stamp_claim`, `sfx_card_deal`, `sfx_tactile_ping`, `sfx_paper_slide_soft`, and `sfx_score_flip` deliberately reused to build a recognizable, cohesive board-game sonic vocabulary.
- **Zero Redundant Shutters:** Both on-screen camera shutters (`S06_SH01` at `f983–f985` and `S08_SH02_V2` at `f2244–f2247`) already exist in the original recordings and are preserved rather than doubled.
- **Dialogue-First Timing:** Every high-impact SFX (`f233`, `f249`, `f708`, `f1295`, `f1378` pre-lap, `f1622`, `f1678`, `f1718–f1844`) is placed either inside verified quiet pockets, pre-lapped `3 frames` ahead of speech, or tuned to dry tactile transients that avoid masking Indonesian consonants (`1.5–4 kHz`).
